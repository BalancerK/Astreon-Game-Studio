class GestureRecognizer#

class GestureRecognizer

Gestures from raw pointers: tap, double tap, long press, swipe, one- and two-finger pan, pinch and rotate. It works on plain numbers (an id, a position, a time in milliseconds), so a touch screen, a pen, a mouse, a test and a replay all feed it the same way; attachGestures connects a page.

One pointer: a short press without moving is a tap (a second one close in time and place is a tap with count: 2); held still it becomes a longpress; dragged it is a pan, and a quick, long enough flick that ends the pan is also a swipe. Two pointers: a pan of their centre, a pinch (the scale of the distance between them) and a rotate (the turn of the line between them). Putting a second finger down cancels a tap in progress.

Gesture objects: {type, x, y, ...} where x and y are where it happened (the centre, for two fingers). pan, pinch and rotate carry a phase of 'start', 'move' or 'end'.

class InputCore#

class InputCore

Device-independent input. You describe what the player can DO (actions: jump, fire, move, look) and what controls do it (bindings: a key, a button, a stick, an on-screen control); the game reads actions once per frame. The core knows nothing about the browser: adapters (see devices.js) feed it raw events, and tests can feed it directly.

Reading: call update(dt) once per frame, then held('fire'), pressed('jump'), released(...), value('zoom'), vector('move'). Vectors from a pointing device or a stick-as-look are per-frame turns in radians (right and up are +); vectors from keys or sticks are unit vectors (length at most 1).

StrokeSample#

A stroke as a pen (or finger, or mouse) draws it: a list of samples with position, pressure, tilt and time. StrokeRecorder turns a stream of pointer positions into strokes (thinning out samples that barely moved, smoothing jitter, keeping the high-rate samples a pen reports between frames), and penWidth turns pressure into a brush width. The pieces take plain numbers, so a test or a replay can draw too; attachPenStrokes connects a page.

Type: {x: number, y: number, pressure: number, tiltX: number, tiltY: number, t: number}
